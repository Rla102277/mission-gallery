Behind the Lens

Photography is where artistry converges with storytelling, where technical mastery serves emotional truth.

I started chasing the places that couldn’t be scrolled past. The Infinite Arch is built on the belief that the most honest photographs aren’t taken — they’re waited for. They come to the photographer who arrives early, stays late, and returns when the light changes.

Who understands that the arch in the rock and the arch of a life are describing the same passage: a threshold between what was and what’s possible.

This is photography as contemplation. Landscape as mirror. Light as metaphor. The goal isn’t the image that impresses, but the one that’s irreplaceable — the one I had to be cold for, had to wait for, had to raise the camera toward the impossible light and believe something was there before I could see it.

Beyond the camera: I’m a marathon runner who lost 100 pounds and found a second life in the outdoors. I bring that same transformation philosophy into the WE CLIMB initiative and the Hope Hike program — because the summit and the shutter share the same lesson.

The Process

How the work gets made.

01

Scout & Research

Weeks of topo maps, tide charts, and sunrise angles before I ever leave the house. The best light isn’t found — it’s predicted.

02

Arrive & Wait

Hours before dawn, hours after sunset. The tripod stays planted while the conditions evolve. Patience is the first creative decision.

03

Edit & Sequence

From 429 frames to 8 series. The edit is where the narrative emerges — where individual images become a body of work.
