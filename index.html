The Vision

The arch frames what lies beyond.
Every image is a gateway.

Most of us spend our lives just outside the frame — scrolling past the image, saving it for later, never quite arriving. I started chasing the places that couldn't be scrolled past.

Photography is where light becomes language and landscape becomes mirror. The most honest photographs aren't taken — they're waited for. Cold mornings, uncertain skies, the moment you raise the camera toward impossible light and believe something is there before you can see it.

The Near Distance

The land you drive past every day without stopping. Palo Duro Canyon at blue hour. The Llano Estacado in winter light. Texas landscape is undershot and overlooked — that's exactly why it belongs in the work.

The Approach

How the photographs get made.

01

Research & Arrival

Locations are studied before they're visited. Weather patterns, golden hour windows, seasonal light — the expedition plan is built around the image, not the schedule. Arriving prepared means arriving open.

02

Medium Format Patience

The GFX system doesn't reward spray-and-pray. 102 megapixels asks you to slow down, choose your moment, and mean it. Technical mastery is what frees you to respond to what you couldn't plan for.

03

The Wait

The honest photograph isn't taken — it's waited for. Sometimes that means three hours in the cold. Sometimes it means coming back on the fifth day. The image that earns its place always knew you were willing to wait.
